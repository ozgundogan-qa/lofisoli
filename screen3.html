Our Mission

Gear That Gives Back.

It started with a simple idea on a backpacking trip through the Pacific Northwest: gear shouldn't just survive the elements; it should help preserve them.

We noticed trails littered with microplastics and disposable gear. We decided to build something different. A brand rooted in circularity, durability, and a deep respect for the outdoors.

Every bracelet we weave is a promise. A promise to use recycled materials, to minimize our carbon footprint, and to donate a portion of every sale to rewilding projects across the globe.
